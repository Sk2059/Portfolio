ABOUT ME

I'm a Full-Stack AI Engineer based in Biratnagar, Nepal. I spent from January 2023 until 2025 as a Full Stack Python Developer at ClickDigitals, and I'm currently open to new opportunities while completing my B.Sc. CSIT at Tribhuvan University and actively building side projects.

Day to day, that means designing REST APIs with Django REST Framework, building JWT/role-based authentication, optimizing PostgreSQL queries, and shipping React frontends that talk to real backend systems — real estate platforms, employee management systems, and hospital booking tools used by actual clients.

Right now I'm channeling that into independent work: extending my stack toward FastAPI, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM-powered agents through hands-on side projects — treating AI not as a gimmick, but as another production layer that has to be reliable, tested, and maintainable.
